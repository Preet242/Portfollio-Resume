♠ Poker Table Monitor

How this works

1

Add your table

Pick saved profiles or add new players, in the order they're sitting. Set buy-in and blind amounts.

2

Press Next Hand

Dealer, small blind and big blind rotate automatically. Turn on Special Hands to randomly throw in an Omaha (or whatever you add) hand once per orbit — never the same seat twice in a row.

3

Track buy-ins

Tap + Buy-in any time someone rebuys. This tracks money in, not chip stacks — you count chips yourself at the table.

4

Put it on the TV

Open this same page on the laptop connected to your TV, and set its Display Mode (Settings) to "Poker View" — it fullscreens the clean table. Open the page again on your phone or tablet and use it normally as the controller. Both stay in sync automatically over the internet — no cables between them needed. On the TV device, tap ✕ in the corner to get back out of Poker View if you need to.

5

End Game

Enter everyone's final chip count and it works out the minimum number of payments to settle up.
